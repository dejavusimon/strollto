Things to do in Bloor West Village

A free, self-guided walk · ~3.6 km · ~47 min of walking · 10 stops · Step-free (from the City)

Bloor West Village sits along Bloor Street West between Jane and Runnymede stations. This guide follows the City of Toronto’s free StrollTO walk: ten stops, from a school that opened in 1927 to a small park whose plaque marks a world first.

Ready to walk

A village by the stream, a firehall and a movie palace

Starts at Runnymede Collegiate Institute, 569 Jane Street. Ends at Neil McLellan Park, about 100 m from Runnymede station.

See the stroll

Stop descriptions: City of Toronto StrollTO.

Getting there

Jane station is on Line 2 (the green subway line), about a 22-minute walk south of Stop 1. Take the 35 Jane bus north, or walk up Jane Street. The walk ends about 100 m from Runnymede station, also on Line 2.

Questions people ask

Is the walk free?
Yes. Buying anything at the shops on Bloor Street West is up to you.
How long does it take?
About 47 minutes of walking over about 3.6 km, plus time at the 10 stops. Both are estimates.
Is it accessible?
The City says this walk is entirely accessible on paved streets and paths, and all points of interest are viewable from street level.
Are there washrooms?
Not checked yet. We’ll add public washrooms after the site visit.
Where does it start and end?
It starts at Runnymede Collegiate Institute, 569 Jane Street, and ends at Neil McLellan Park, 263 Beresford Avenue.
