# How to try the StrollTO prototype (about 5 minutes)

**What is StrollTO?** StrollTO is the City of Toronto's set of free, self-guided walks through neighbourhoods across the city. Each stroll is a short route of stops, such as a historic building, a mural or a park, with a few lines about each one. This prototype is a companion for those walks: you plan a stroll on a computer, then walk it with your phone.

Open the link on a computer first. No account is needed.

1. **Find a stroll.** There are 10 guided walks. On the website's Explore page, search for a neighbourhood or a place, answer "What do you enjoy?", or pick a card under *Featured this week*. Then press **See this stroll**.
2. **Plan it.** Under **Plan your walk**, pick a shorter plan, untick stops, or drag stops into your own order. The time and distance update.
3. **Send it to your phone.** Press **Send to my phone** and scan the QR code with your phone's camera. The stroll opens on your phone, with your plan. No phone handy? Click the code to preview the phone on the computer.
4. **Walk it.** On the phone, press **Start stroll**, then **Next stop**. Try **Skip this stop** once. On the last stop, press **Finish stroll**. You don't need to be at the stops.
5. **Look around.** On the website, try **Map** and **Compare** in the top menu.

**Start over** (top right) resets everything, and **How to try it** shows these steps again. Opening the link on a phone goes straight to the phone app.

## Good to know

- This is a prototype for review. It is not a City of Toronto service.
- Stops and stories come from the City of Toronto's StrollTO open data. Titles, "Look for" lines, featured picks, community posts and the walk buddy are team drafts or samples.
- Photo credits are still being confirmed.
- Nothing you do is sent anywhere. Your progress on the phone stays in that phone's browser.
- Tell us what worked, what was confusing and what's missing.
